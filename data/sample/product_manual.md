## 1. Overview

The Device X1 is a compact desktop sensor used for monitoring environmental
conditions in a workspace. It measures temperature, relative humidity, and
particulate concentration, and reports readings over a local serial or USB
connection.

## 2. Warranty

The Device X1 is covered by a **24-month** warranty from the date of delivery.

The warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship under normal use. The
manufacturer will repair or replace a defective unit within the warranty
period at no charge.

### 2.1 Warranty exclusions

The following are **not** covered by the warranty:

- Physical damage from impact, liquid ingress, or incorrect voltage supply.
- Normal degradation of the particulate filter, which is a consumable and is
  replaced on a 12-month schedule.
- Loss of data caused by a power interruption longer than 5 seconds.
- Any unit whose serial number has been removed or altered.
- Damage arising from use with a third-party power supply that does not meet
  the specified output tolerance.

## 3. Installation

1. Place the unit on a stable, level surface away from direct sunlight.
2. Connect the supplied USB-C cable to the host device.
3. Open the companion application and select **Device X1** from the detected
   devices list.
4. Allow a 60-second warm-up period before taking calibration readings.

## 4. Calibration

Calibration requires a reference thermometer with a stated accuracy of
±0.2 °C. Place the reference sensor 100 mm from the intake aperture and follow
the in-application calibration wizard. A calibration record is stored locally
on the host device.
